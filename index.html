About Salutar

Slow, natural, unmistakably Australian.

Salutar began with one simple belief — that a candle should be honest.

No synthetic fillers, no shortcuts. Just natural soy wax, cotton wicks, and fragrance oils chosen for the way they sit quietly in a room rather than shout across it.

Every candle is hand poured in small batches in Melbourne, inspired by the native plants, coastlines and quiet corners of the Australian landscape.
